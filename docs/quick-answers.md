# Quick answers

**A panel says "No readings in this period".** Nothing arrived from that source for the dates you chose. It does not mean the value was zero. If it lasts more than a day, contact your LDS account manager.

**A page sits on "Loading".** Give it ten seconds, then reload. Long periods take longer.

**Two figures for the same thing do not match.** They probably count different things, cover different dates, or one is an estimate. Read the sentence under each. See [Measured, estimated or written](getting-started/measured-estimated-written.md).

**The answered percentage looks too good.** It is a share of the calls that reached the queue, not of all calls. See [The advice line](telephony/advice-line.md).

**Electricity cost shows £0.00.** The tariff has not been set up for that site. Send a recent bill to your LDS account manager.

**The handyperson figures look very low.** Stirling counts jobs booked in Handy Andy. Visits kept only in the Outlook diary are not counted.

**A building shows "no environment sensor".** That building reports electricity only. Nothing is broken.

**The written summary says something the figures do not.** Trust the figures. The summary is generated text. Tell your LDS account manager so it can be looked at.

**Can I change a figure?** No. Stirling is read-only. Correct the record in the system it came from, and Stirling will follow.

**Can I export anything?** The attendance record on the Operations page can be downloaded as a CSV file. It contains personal data, so store it carefully.

**Who can see our figures?** Access is given person by person, and each account is tied to one organisation. Ask your LDS account manager for the current list of people with access to yours.
