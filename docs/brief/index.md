# Brief

**One page, one decision.**

The Brief is the page to open first. It gives you the period in a few short paragraphs, then the figures those paragraphs were written from.

## The written summary

At the top is a short summary covering each service in turn, ending with where attention is most needed.

It is written by a language model from the figures below it, and is labelled **Generated text, not a measurement**. Read it to get your bearings, then check what matters against the figures. See [Measured, estimated or written](../getting-started/measured-estimated-written.md).

## The figures

Under the summary, each service has a block of four figures. The blocks you see depend on which services your organisation has connected.

| Block | Figures | More detail on |
|---|---|---|
| **Advice line** | Calls, Answered, Busiest hour, Talk time | [Telephony](../telephony/advice-line.md) |
| **Out-of-hours assistant** | Calls answered, Outside office hours, Call-backs waiting, Safeguarding raised | [Telephony](../telephony/the-assistant.md) |
| **Footcare** | Taken, Appointments, Unsold slots, Unsold value (est.) | [Finance](../finance/service-income.md) |
| **Home support** | Visits, Hours, Revenue, Net margin (est.) | [Finance](../finance/service-income.md) and [Operations](../operations/home-support.md) |
| **Handyperson** | Jobs, Chargeable hours, Charged, Materials | [Finance](../finance/service-income.md) |
| **Admin saved (est.)** | Hours, Cost avoided, From events | [Finance](../finance/assistant-savings.md) |

Figures marked **(est.)** are estimates. The page they come from states the assumptions behind them.

## Reading "Answered"

The answered percentage is a share of the calls that reached the queue and have a clear outcome, not of every call. The Telephony page gives the numbers behind it. See [The advice line](../telephony/advice-line.md).
