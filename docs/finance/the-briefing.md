# The briefing

**Page: Finance, foot of the page**

The briefing is a short note, with a headline, on the most significant change in the period. For example, it may tell you that one service's takings are down on the previous period and suggest what to do.

It is written by a language model from the figures on the page, and is labelled **Generated text, not a measurement**.

## How to use it

1. Read the headline. It points you at the change most worth your attention.
2. Find the figures it quotes on the page and check them. The briefing may compare with the previous period, which is not shown in the tiles, so a figure may be unfamiliar.
3. Treat the recommendations as ideas for discussion. The model does not know your clients, your capacity, your pricing policy or your charitable objects. A suggestion to discount a service or run a campaign is a prompt for a conversation, not advice.

!!! warning "Do not act on the briefing alone"
    Decisions about prices, staffing or services belong with your managers and trustees. Use the briefing to choose what to look at, then decide from the measured figures and your own knowledge.
