# Electricity cost

**Page: Finance, top**

Electricity cost for each site over the period, worked out at that site's own tariff. The cost is calculated for every half hour as the data arrives and added up here, so a time-of-use tariff is handled properly.

The large tile is the **incoming supply**, which is the bill you actually pay. It shows the cost, the units used and the all-in price per unit.

The smaller tiles are the **sub-meters**. Each carries its own standing charge in the calculation, so adding them together would overstate what you pay. Use them to see how the bill splits between buildings, not to total it.

## If the cost shows £0.00

If a site shows units used but a cost of £0.00, its tariff has not been set up in Stirling yet. The usage is real; the price is missing. Send your LDS account manager a recent bill showing the unit rates and the standing charge, and the costs will fill in, including for past periods.

## Using it

- Check the all-in price per unit against your bill. If they differ by more than a little, the tariff held in Stirling may be out of date, for example after a contract renewal.
- Use the split between buildings when you recharge a tenant or a project for its share.
