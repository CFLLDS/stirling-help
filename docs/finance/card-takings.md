# Card takings

**Page: Finance, Card takings**

Card reader and online takings over the period. Totals only; no cardholder data is held.

## The three tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Taken** | Total taken, the provider's fees, and the net amount you receive |
| **Payments** | Number of payments, with refunds and failed payments |
| **Average payment** | Taken, divided by the number of payments |

## Four ways of splitting the same money

Under the tiles are four lines. Each adds up to the same total, cut a different way.

| Line | Answers |
|---|---|
| **By service** | Which service earned it |
| **By location** | Where it was taken |
| **What was sold** | Which items people paid for |
| **How it was taken** | Card reader, or online |

The number in brackets after each amount is the number of payments.

## Payments with no description

If a payment is taken without choosing an item, it cannot be attributed to a service. Stirling groups these together, shows their total, and repeats it on the Operations page as a [record-keeping breach](../operations/sla-breaches.md).

If this figure is a large share of your takings, the split by service is not reliable until it comes down. Ask everyone who uses a reader to choose the item first.

## Failed payments

A failed payment is a card that was declined or a payment that was abandoned. Nothing was taken. A high number at one location may point to a poor signal for the reader there.

## Reconciling

The net figure should match what arrives in your bank from the card provider for the same dates, allowing for the provider's payout delay. Cash, cheques and bank transfers are not included here.
