# Capacity not being sold

**Page: Finance, Capacity not being sold**

This section looks at your home support roster and finds paid time that has no visit booked against it.

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sellable hours** | Hours in the roster with no visit booked |
| **At the charge rate** | What those hours would bring in if every one were sold. An upper limit, not a forecast |
| **Already paid for** | Contracted staff with unsold paid time. You are carrying this cost whether or not the hours are sold |
| **Want more work** | Zero-hours staff who have offered availability that is not being used |

## Which hours to sell first

Sell the **already paid for** hours first. They cost you nothing extra, so everything you charge for them goes to margin.

Hours from staff who **want more work** cost you their pay when you use them, so they are worth less to the organisation, but offering them keeps good carers from looking elsewhere.

## Using it

When a new enquiry comes in, look here before you say there is a waiting list. If the figures are regularly high, the constraint is demand or how the roster is built, not the number of carers.
