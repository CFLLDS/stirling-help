# Service income and margin

**Page: Finance, middle**

Each connected service has its own section. They do not all show the same thing, because Stirling only shows a margin where it has been given the costs.

## Footcare income

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Taken** | Card payments for footcare, with the number of payments and the average |
| **Per clinic day** | Takings divided by the number of days with takings |
| **Forward booked** | Appointments already in the diary, multiplied by the average payment |

This is **income only**. There are no clinic costs in Stirling's cost assumptions, such as the practitioner's rate, the room or consumables, so nothing is subtracted. To turn it into a margin, give those costs to your LDS account manager.

On the Brief you will also see **unsold slots** and their estimated value: clinic time that was available and not booked, priced at the average payment.

## Home support P&L

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | Hours multiplied by the charge rate, split into delivered and still rostered |
| **All-in cost** | Direct pay, on-cost, a share of overheads, and the rostering system's licence |
| **Gross margin** | Revenue less direct pay and on-cost |
| **Net margin** | Revenue less all-in cost, also shown per visit |

Under the tiles, the page sets out the whole calculation in one paragraph: the hourly pay, the on-cost percentage, the overhead percentage, the licence fee, and what is left out, such as mileage, shift premiums and one-off adjustments.

!!! warning "A safe-pass estimate, not audited"
    Read the assumptions paragraph before you use these figures. If the pay rate or charge rate shown is not yours, the margin is not yours either. Tell your LDS account manager and it will be corrected.

## Handyperson financials

Charged work from the handyperson service: the amount charged with its net and VAT, labour, travel, materials, and what is booked ahead. A line underneath splits jobs by who paid and at which rate.

VAT is charged per job, not at a blended rate. Some work is zero-rated, so the VAT shown will be less than 20% of the net. That is correct.

This section counts jobs booked in Handy Andy. Work kept only in the Outlook diary is not included until it is turned into a Handy Andy job.
