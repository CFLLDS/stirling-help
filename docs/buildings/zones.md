# Temperature and humidity

**Page: Buildings, Zones**

Each building with an environment sensor shows its temperature **now**, the range it has moved through today, and its humidity. This section always shows today, whatever reporting period you have chosen.

## Reading the temperature

| You see | Think about |
|---|---|
| Below about 18°C while people are in | Too cold for older visitors and for staff sitting still. Check the heating and the timer |
| Around 19 to 23°C | Comfortable for most people |
| Well above that in the heating season | The heating may be working against open windows, or the controls are set too high |
| A very narrow range across the whole day | The heating or cooling may be running through the night. Check the timer |

If your buildings are used by older people, remember that a room that feels fine to someone moving around can be cold for someone sitting for two hours.

## Reading the humidity

| Relative humidity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| About 40% to 60% | Comfortable, and a healthy range for a building |
| Above 60% for long periods | Condensation and mould become more likely, especially on cold walls and in store rooms. Ventilate, and check for a source of damp |
| Below 30% | Dry air. Uncomfortable for eyes and throats, but not harmful to the building |

## "No environment sensor"

A building that shows **no environment sensor** reports electricity only. Nothing is broken; there is no sensor there to read. If you would like one, speak to your LDS account manager.
