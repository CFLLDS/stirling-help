# Network

**Page: Buildings, Network**

This section reads your Wi-Fi and network switches. It is sampled every half hour, and the source line shows the time of the last sample.

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Devices on the network** | Everything connected right now, split into wired and wireless |
| **Network equipment** | How many switches and access points are online, and whether any updates are outstanding |
| **Internet** | How long the connection has been up, and how much it is carrying at the moment |
| A tile per building | Devices in that building, with how many are **always on** |

## A device is not a person

A device is anything with a network address: a printer, a phone, a laptop, a card reader, a visitor's tablet. So the count is equipment and people together. It is not a headcount, and it cannot tell you who is in the building.

The tile gives you a better clue. It separates devices that were already on before the building opened from those that joined during the day. The first group is your equipment. The second rises and falls as people arrive and leave.

## What to act on

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Equipment showing fewer online than expected | Part of a building may have no Wi-Fi. Check the equipment has power, then contact your IT support |
| Updates outstanding | Ask your IT support to schedule them |
| Internet uptime of only a few hours | The connection dropped recently. If it keeps happening, raise it with your provider |
| Many more always-on devices than you expect | Something has been left connected, or there is equipment nobody remembers. Worth a walk round |
