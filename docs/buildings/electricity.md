# Electricity

**Page: Buildings, top**

## The incoming supply and the sub-meters

The first, larger tile is your **incoming supply**: everything the site drew from the grid in the period, with the average per day.

The smaller tiles underneath are **sub-meters** inside that supply, usually one for each building or part of a building. Each shows its use, its share of the site total and its daily average.

!!! warning "Do not add them together"
    The sub-meters sit inside the incoming supply. They are not extra usage on top. Adding the large tile to the small ones would count the same electricity twice.

## The reconciliation line

Under the tiles is a line comparing the sub-meters' total with the incoming supply, and giving the difference. A small difference is normal, because the meters are read separately. If the difference is more than a few per cent, something on site is not sub-metered, or a meter needs checking. Tell your LDS account manager.

## Days reported

"28 of 28 days reported" tells you the feed was complete. If days are missing, the total is low because data is missing, not because less was used.

## Using it

- Compare a building's daily average with its occupied hours on the Operations page. A building that uses nearly as much on a closed day as an open one has something left on.
- Look at the daily chart for the weekend. The lowest days show your baseload: fridges, servers, anything that never switches off. That is usually where the easy savings are.
- Compare the same period a year apart using **Custom…**, once you have a year of data.
