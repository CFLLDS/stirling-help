# Record-keeping breaches

**Page: Operations, SLA breaches**

This panel shows records that cost admin time to put right. It measures two things.

## 1. Check-in and check-out evidence

For each carer, Stirling works out the share of visits that have **both** a check-in and a check-out recorded. Anyone below 95% is listed, with the number of visits and the number of missing records.

!!! note "This measures the record, not the visit"
    A visit can be delivered perfectly well and still appear here because it was not logged at both ends. Treat the list as a prompt for a supportive conversation about using the app, not as evidence of missed care.

Carers with fewer than ten visits in the period are not scored. One missed record out of two is not a pattern.

**What to do.** Speak to the carer. The usual causes are a phone with no signal at the client's home, forgetting to check out, or a shared login. Fix the cause, and correct the missing records in your rostering system while people still remember the visit.

**Why it matters.** Check-in and check-out are your evidence that a visit happened if an invoice is ever questioned. A visit with one end missing is harder to defend.

## 2. Card payments with no description

When a card payment is taken without choosing an item or typing a description, nobody can tell afterwards which service it was for. Stirling counts these and shows their total value.

**What to do.** Remind everyone who uses a card reader to choose the item from the list before taking the payment. It takes two seconds at the time and saves finance going back through the day to work out what each payment was.
