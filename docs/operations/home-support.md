# Home support visits

**Page: Operations, "Where the visits are" and "People this period"**

## Where the visits are

A simple map of home support and handyperson visits by area. It is deliberately coarse:

- visits are grouped into squares of roughly two kilometres
- any square with fewer than three visits is left out, and the page tells you how many visits that hides
- the line underneath lists the busiest postcode areas

Use it to see where your services reach and where they do not, for example when planning recruitment or a funding bid. It cannot be used to find a person's home.

## People this period

Six tiles taken from your rostering system:

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Appointments | Appointments delivered in the period |
| Hours delivered | Actual hours, from check-in and check-out where recorded |
| Carers active | People with at least one appointment |
| Average visit | Average length of a visit, in minutes |
| Cancellations | Share of appointments cancelled |
| Postcode areas | How many areas you covered |

If nothing is rostered for the coming weeks, a line tells you: **Nothing booked ahead.** That usually means the forward roster has not been published yet, not that there is no work.

## Punctuality

Three tiles show the share of visits that started **on time**, **early** or **late**, out of the visits with a recorded check-in.

Read these with care. A carer who arrives early because the previous client was not in is counted as early. Look for a pattern over several periods before you act.

## Evidence

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Visits fully evidenced | Visits with both a check-in and a check-out |
| One end only | Visits with only one of the two. Harder to defend if the invoice is questioned |
| No record | Visits with neither |

## Export the attendance record

Select **Export attendance record (CSV)** to download the check-in and check-out record for the period. Use it when a client, a family or a commissioner questions an invoice.

!!! warning "The export contains personal data"
    Each line carries a time and a location. The coordinates are in the export and not on the page for that reason. Store the file with your other personal records and delete it when you have finished.
