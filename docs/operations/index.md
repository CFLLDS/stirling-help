# Operations

**What is happening now, and what needs a person.**

Operations is the service manager's page. It runs from the most urgent at the top to the most detailed at the bottom.

## The page at a glance

**Four headline figures.**

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Building hours | Hours your buildings were in use in the period |
| Appointments | Home support appointments delivered |
| Evidenced | The share of visits with both a check-in and a check-out recorded |
| Footcare | Footcare appointments booked |

**Then, in order:**

| Section | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Issues | Things that need a person, and things worth knowing | [Issues and welfare alerts](issues.md) |
| Building hours | When each building was actually in use, day by day | [Buildings](../buildings/index.md) |
| SLA breaches | Records that cost admin time to put right | [Record-keeping breaches](sla-breaches.md) |
| Where the visits are | Visits by area, without addresses | [Home support visits](home-support.md) |
| People this period | Appointments, hours, carers, punctuality and evidence | [Home support visits](home-support.md) |
| Footcare | How full the clinic diary is, back and forward | [Footcare and handyperson](footcare-and-handyperson.md) |
| Handyperson bookings | When the handyperson is booked | [Footcare and handyperson](footcare-and-handyperson.md) |

## Building hours

Stirling works out from each building's own data which hours it was in use. Each building has a small chart of occupied hours per day, its average, and its busiest day. "28 of 28 days reported" tells you the feed was complete.

Use it to see whether your opening hours match how the building is really used.
