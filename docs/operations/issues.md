# Issues and welfare alerts

**Page: Operations, Issues**

The Issues panel lists things that need a person, and things worth knowing. Each line has a tag on the left saying what kind of action it needs.

## Welfare alerts

A line tagged **CALL** and headed **Welfare alert** has been raised by the welfare check that runs on the appointment feed. It means something in the pattern of a client's appointments suggests somebody should check on them.

What to do:

1. Find out which client the alert relates to in your rostering system. Stirling does not name clients.
2. Ring the client or their carer, following your own welfare procedure.
3. Record what you did in your own case records.

!!! warning "Stirling is not an emergency system"
    A welfare alert is a prompt to make a call. It is not monitored out of hours and it does not contact anyone for you. If you believe someone is at immediate risk, call 999. If you believe an adult is at risk of abuse or neglect, follow your safeguarding policy.

## If the list is empty

Nothing needs a person right now. That is the normal state.

## If the same alert stays for days

Alerts clear when the pattern that raised them clears. If one stays after you have acted on it, tell your LDS account manager.
