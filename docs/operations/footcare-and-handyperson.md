# Footcare and handyperson

**Page: Operations, lower half**

## Footcare

Appointment counts from your clinic diary. Counts only; no patient data leaves the clinical system.

- The **heat grid** shows booked appointments by day and hour, looking back over the period and forward over the coming weeks. See [Read a heat grid](heat-grids.md).
- The **tiles** underneath show bookings by appointment type, for example toenail cutting, treatment and initial assessment.

Use it to spot clinic sessions that are regularly half empty, and to see whether the forward book is filling. The money side is on [Finance](../finance/service-income.md).

## Handyperson bookings

When the handyperson service is booked. Counts and minutes only; no customer, address or job note leaves that service.

Under the grid is a line with the number of bookings, the chargeable hours and any cancellations. A second line tells you if nothing is booked for the coming weeks.

!!! note "Why this may look low"
    This panel counts jobs booked in Handy Andy. If your handyperson diary is still mostly kept in Outlook, those visits are not counted here until they are turned into Handy Andy jobs.
