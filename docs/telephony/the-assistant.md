# The assistant

**Page: Telephony, middle**

The assistant answers calls and text messages when your team cannot, for example out of hours or when all lines are busy. It can answer common questions, take a message and arrange a call back. It hands over to a person whenever it should.

## Answered by the assistant

A heat grid of the calls the assistant took, laid out the same way as the call volume grid above it so that the two read together.

The line underneath splits the calls into those taken **outside office hours** and those taken **during** them. It also states the office hours Stirling is using. Calls outside those hours would otherwise have rung out.

If the office hours shown are wrong, tell your LDS account manager, because the split depends on them.

## SMS assistant

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Conversations** | Conversations the assistant handled in the period |
| **Resolved** | Ended without needing a call back or a cancellation |
| **Asked for a call back** | The person wanted to speak to someone. These become the call-back list |
| **Safeguarding flagged** | Something in the conversation raised a safeguarding concern. Each one was escalated to a person |

## Safeguarding flags

When the assistant hears or reads something that suggests a person may be at risk, it flags the conversation and escalates it to a person in your organisation. It does not wait for someone to open Stirling.

The tile is a count, so that managers and trustees can see that flags are being raised and handled. The detail is held with your safeguarding lead, not on this page.
