# The advice line

**Page: Telephony, top half**

## The four tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Calls** | Every call into the main number in the period, with active days, the daily average and the busiest hour |
| **Answered** | The share of calls that were routed to a person, out of those that reached the queue |
| **Abandoned** | Callers who reached the queue and hung up before anyone answered |
| **Time on calls** | Total time spent talking, and the average per call |

## Reading "Answered" properly

The sentence under the tile matters. It reads something like: "481 routed of 513 calls that reached the queue. The other calls have no clean outcome recorded and are not counted either way."

So the percentage is **not** a share of all calls. Many calls never reach the queue: internal transfers, calls to a direct extension, calls that end in the menu, and so on. The phone system does not record a clean outcome for those, so Stirling leaves them out and does not guess.

When you quote the figure, quote it as "of calls that reached the queue".

## Abandoned calls

A few abandoned calls are normal; people misdial or change their mind. Look at the trend. If it rises, compare the busiest hour with your staffing at that hour.

## When people call

| Panel | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **When people call** | See calls by hour of the day. The bars run midnight to midnight |
| **Busiest day** | See calls by day of the week |
| **Call volume by hour** | See every day and hour in one grid. See [Read a heat grid](../operations/heat-grids.md) |

Put together, these tell you when to protect staff time for the phones, and when it is safe to schedule meetings and training.
