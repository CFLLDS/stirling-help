# Work the call-back list

**Page: Telephony, Call-backs waiting**

People who asked the assistant for a call back appear here, **oldest first**. Each line shows the phone number, when they asked, and sometimes the service they asked about and a line on what they need.

## Work the list

1. Start at the top. The person at the top has waited longest.
2. Read the line, if there is one, so you know what they want before you ring.
3. Ring them. If there is no answer, try again later the same day and at a different time the next day.
4. Record the call where you normally would, for example in your case records.

The list always shows the present moment. It does not follow the reporting period.

## Keep the list short

A person who asked for a call and did not get one is worse off than a person who never got through, because they are now waiting. Agree in your team:

- who checks the list, and when. First thing each morning works well, because most requests arrive out of hours
- how quickly you aim to ring back. One working day is a sensible promise
- who covers when that person is away

!!! warning "This list is personal data"
    It shows phone numbers and sometimes a little about what the person needs. Do not screenshot it, and do not leave it open on a shared screen.
