# What the assistant costs

**Page: Telephony, foot of the page**

This section sets the assistant's bill for the current month against the staff time it avoided.

| Tile | Meaning | Kind of figure |
|---|---|---|
| **Billed** | The contact-centre bill for the month so far, with the number of line items | Measured |
| **Staff time avoided** | Hours your team would have spent handling the same calls and messages, and what that is worth at loaded cost | Estimated |
| **Net saving** | The value of time avoided, less the bill | Estimated |

The line underneath itemises the bill: number rental, text messages out and in, voice calls in and out, and voice synthesis.

## How far to trust it

The bill is measured usage priced on your rate card. The date of the rate card is shown.

The time avoided is **estimated** from that usage; nobody stood with a stopwatch. It is a fair guide to whether the assistant pays for itself, and the page says so plainly. Do not present it as a cash saving: the time is freed for other work, it does not leave the payroll.

This section covers the **current calendar month**, not the reporting period at the top of the page.
