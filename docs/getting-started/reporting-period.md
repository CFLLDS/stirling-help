# Choose the reporting period

Every page has a **Reporting period** list at the top right. It sets the dates for every panel on the page.

| Option | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Last 7 days** | The seven days up to today. Good for a weekly team meeting |
| **Last 28 days** | Four whole weeks up to today. The default, and the fairest for comparing one period with the next, because it always contains four of each weekday |
| **This quarter** | From the start of the current quarter to today. Good for board and funder reports |
| **Custom…** | Any start and end date you choose |

The period stays with you as you move between tabs.

## Things that do not follow the period

A few panels always show the present moment, whatever period you choose:

- temperature and humidity in each building
- the devices on the network right now
- the list of people waiting for a call back

Some panels also look **forward**. Footcare shows appointments already booked for the coming weeks, and the handyperson and home support panels tell you if nothing is booked ahead.

## "Data held from"

Near the top of some pages you will see a line such as "Data held from" followed by a date. That is when Stirling started receiving that feed. If you choose a period that starts earlier, the figures cover only the part of the period that Stirling holds.
