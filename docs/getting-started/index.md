# Getting started

## What Stirling is

Stirling is a read-only view across your organisation. It collects figures from the systems you already run, such as your phone system, your care rostering, your clinic diary, your card readers and the meters and sensors in your buildings, and lays them out on five pages.

Nothing you do in Stirling changes anything in those systems. You cannot break anything by looking.

## Who it is for

| You are | You will mostly use |
|---|---|
| Chief executive or trustee | [Brief](../brief/index.md), then Finance |
| Service manager or coordinator | [Operations](../operations/index.md) and Telephony |
| Finance lead | [Finance](../finance/index.md) |
| Premises or facilities lead | [Buildings](../buildings/index.md) |

## Signing in

You sign in with a link sent to your work email; there is no password to remember. Your account is linked to your organisation, so you only ever see your own organisation's figures. If you cannot sign in, contact your LDS account manager.
